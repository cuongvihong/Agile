MOE Finance System, tender requirements 1, 3, 8, 9

----------------------------------------------------------------------
FOR PM AND PO
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. You estimated about 500 HQ users, 300 schools with about 200 users each
and up to 2 million parents. These are not concurrent users. We propose
about 1,400 staff and 800 parents at normal load, and twice that at peak.
This assumes that 50 percent of staff and 10 percent of parents use the
system each day, in sessions of 30 and 5 minutes. Are these shares and
session lengths reasonable, or how many concurrent users do you expect?
Ref: OQ-17

Q2. Parents may use the portal from 6:00 to 2:00, and usage may rise around
bill payment dates. Which dates are the busiest, and how many times the
normal load do you expect then? We plan to test with 2 times.
Ref: OQ-17

Q3. At peak, how slow may the system be? We plan to accept reads within
3 seconds for 95 percent of requests and 6 seconds for 99 percent, writes
and payments within 5 and 8 seconds, at most 2 percent errors, and recovery
to normal within 5 minutes after the overload. Do you agree?

Q4. What counts as a failed operation: a server error, a timeout, a wrong
result, or all of them? Is 1 percent the most that may fail at normal load?

Q5. Parents are expected to use the portal up to 20 hours a day. We plan an
endurance run of 8 hours at the target load and 2 hours at a small load, and
allow response time and memory to grow at most 20 percent. Do you agree?

Q6. How much will the data grow while the system is in use? We plan to test
with 5 times the size of the mock data.

Q7. Will the system run on more than one server? If not, we will not test
scalability.

Q8. After how many minutes of inactivity should a user be signed out? We
propose 30 minutes.

FOR PO AND CLIENT

Q9. What are the allowed format, length and range of each field users type
in: addresses, contact numbers, names, fee data and payment amounts? The BA
analysis assumes mobile numbers of 8 digits starting with 8 or 9 and postal
codes of 6 digits. Please confirm these.

Q10. At closure you expect no pending transactions and the balance to go to
CPF. What happens if a pending transaction still exists when an account
reaches age 16, and what are the interface and the checks for the transfer
to CPF? Ref: OQ-13

Q11. Who creates the payable charges, from which source, and does the system
create them in bulk? For Sprint 1, may we use a test charge dataset?
Ref: OQ-04, OQ-10

Q12. Which fee components carry GST, what are the rate and the rounding rule,
and from which date does a change of rate apply, including to charges
already created? Ref: OQ-10

Q13. Students without an Education Account pay from their own funds. Is
online payment their only option, and are there other restrictions on
payment from balance? Ref: OQ-11

Q14. Where do the parent to child relationships come from, and can more than
one guardian see the same child? Ref: OQ-02

Q15. Parents are expected to use the portal from 6:00 to 2:00, and the
system is not stated to be closed from 2:00 to 6:00. When does the account
creation and closure job run, and may it run while parents are using the
system? Ref: OQ-06

Q16. What are the limits on sign in attempts and on payment requests per
user, and over what period of time do they apply?

Q17. How many students, parents, accounts and transactions does the mock
data hold? Please give the numbers when PB-04 is complete.

Q18. We plan to measure response time from the moment the test tool sends
the request to the moment it receives the full response, without browser
display time. Do you agree?

Q19. Can the speed, success and failure behaviour of the simulated SingPass
login and the mock payment provider be fixed before we start measuring and
stay the same in every round? Is a sandbox available for SingPass, for
online payment and for CPF? Ref: OQ-03, OQ-11, OQ-13

Q20. Can the test environment be sized to carry 1,400 staff and 800 parents
at the same time? If not, what is the largest size you can provide?
